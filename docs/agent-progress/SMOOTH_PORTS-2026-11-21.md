Fidelity of every number below: reported by the upstream repo's own docs or commit messages, not reproduced by us. Treat as `not-tested` on our device.

## 3. Top five by expected gain for us

Basis: ours is 27 fps, GPU about 21 ms per frame at only 40-60% busy, so gaps and a possibly low GPU clock dominate over raw encode cost.

1. **Resolution (#13).** The only mechanism with a measured >25% jump on the same donor userland: 89-91 -> 118-120 scroll at 2.25x fewer pixels, GPU main pass 5 ms. It is a quality trade for you to decide; try 828x1792 first (their XR class, 118 scroll, page flip 110-115).
2. **GPU clock/governor (#1, #2).** annibale: +13 fps (79 -> 93) from 607 to 832 MHz and nothing above. Our GPU at 40-60% busy with 21 ms/frame suggests the clock is the first thing to read, not guess. Read-only step first: log the real a618 clock under load.
3. **AMP topology + spill thresholds + timer coalescing off (#18, #19, #17).** Same SoC and kernel line: about 30 -> 45 fps in interaction windows. Cheap boot-arg level experiments, no GPU risk.
4. **Kill per-submit cache maintenance and merge submissions (#8, #9, #10, #11).** Our flush is 1.3 ms x 3.4 = 4.4 ms/frame of CPU plus about 0.7 ms hand-off per submit and an idle gap after each. DC28088's cache-walk reduction took presents from 23.7 to 29.2/s; that is the nearest analogue to our numbers.
5. **DontCare contents-undefined in the freedreno Metal backend (#5).** 8-12% of the full-screen pass and +7.6 fps in annibale's identical gallium path; verify first that our full-screen pass has a second `RGBA16Float` attachment with `DontCare` load and that tile loads are 20% of pass time.

Runners-up: `ui_lite=1` (#15, free A/B), wallpaper hide test (#14), luma pass check (#16), GPU panel copy (#21), direct scanout (#22, long-term).

## 4. Not verified / limits

- Nothing was reproduced; every number is upstream's own. hakios and annibale fps are synthetic gestures (`rwtouch`/`touch_lat`), counted from swaps or copy-done, not the panel.
- DC28088 NOTES.md is huge (7315 lines); only grep hits and the sections around 2026-11-01, 11-02, 11-11, 11-17, 11-19 were read. Its "UniXNU ~70 fps" is second-hand.
- UniXNU publishes no fps; its device reports were not read beyond grep for performance terms.
- `fb_beat` (#4) semantics are inferred; the annibale kernel source was not read.
- annibale bundles (rate2..rate5, dc1..dc2) change one thing at a time per the doc, but each is a single round, not repeated; hakios itself corrected a single 109 to 89-91.
- Bergamota, joranv1/ios-sailfish and MakrSas/ios-topaz: no fps data located; their entries rest on patch headers and code comments only.
- `gh search commits` filter by date failed on a flag error; discovery was by named repos only.
